## Context

My first role at North West Anglia NHS Foundation Trust was in workforce information, working with HealthRoster data covering 200 to 250 staff and producing management information for service leads and senior managers.

## Problem

The reporting cycle was mostly manual. The same extracts were reshaped by hand each time, checked by hand, and rekeyed into reports. It was slow, and every rekeyed figure was an opportunity for an error that someone would then plan a rota around.

## Approach

- **Automate the boring half.** Python scripts replaced the manual extract and reshape steps across HealthRoster data operations.
- **Fix data at source.** Root cause analysis on anomalies, working with the teams that owned the data rather than correcting the same error every cycle.
- **Point the analysis at money.** Trend analysis on rostering, capacity and leave patterns, aimed at the Trust's Cost Improvement Programme.

## Outcome

A 25 to 30 percent workflow efficiency gain on routine reporting, more consistent data with the manual processing errors gone, and £280k of validated workforce savings delivered through the Cost Improvement Programme.

## Lesson

Automation pays twice. Once in the hours it gives back, and again in the errors it removes from data that people make real decisions on.
