## The plan

Revision notes are usually written once and never read again. Publishing them forces two things, getting the definition right and saying where it shows up on a real programme. Each note takes one area of the qualification, sets out what the Body of Knowledge says, and then sets out where it matches, or does not match, what happens on a £1B+ capital programme.

Notes appear in [Writing](/writing/) as they are finished.
